RPM, measured at 33

Playing vinyl rather than digital music? Records are only recognised at the right speed: a turntable even 2% fast or slow sounds fine but isn't matched. Either check yours runs at 33.3 RPM (a turntable speed app, with the phone on the platter) and adjust the turntable, or enter the speed it measures here and clips are corrected before they're sent. Leave it at 33.33 for radio, streaming or an accurate turntable.

If your records are listed on Discogs, each song is matched to the record you own: its album, year and cover, and which side and track it is. Enter your Discogs username and a personal access token (Discogs → Settings → Developers → Generate new token), from your computer (the one running the controller, or one it trusts; not a phone):

The latest 50 are shown. If a track was learned under the wrong name, forget it and it will be learned again the next time it plays.

Once a record is identified it waits for the quiet gap before the next track (or the track's expected end), so each track costs one request. Tracks it has learned from an earlier play are recognised here and cost nothing. It won't try while Spotify is playing, stops at the monthly limits, and switches itself off after the time set.
