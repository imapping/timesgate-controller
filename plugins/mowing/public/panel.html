Shows which lawns and areas are due for mowing, from Mowing Tracker. Create an API key on its Setup page (it's shown only once), then paste it here from your computer (the one running the controller, or one it trusts; not a phone):

Screens: a map of the property with each area in its status colour · the area to mow next · how many are overdue, due soon and up to date · the next few areas and when it was last mowed. Colours: red overdue, amber due today or tomorrow, green up to date, grey never mowed. It checks every 10 minutes. The key stays on the controller; nothing can be changed in Mowing Tracker from here.
