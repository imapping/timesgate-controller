Screens during a game: the away team and its runs · the home team and its runs · the inning, runners, outs and count · who's batting and pitching · runs, hits and errors. Before a game: the teams, when it starts (your time) and the series; after it: the final score and the next game. It checks every 30 seconds during a game, so the screens are up to about a minute behind the broadcast. Scores from MLB's public stats feed, for personal use.
