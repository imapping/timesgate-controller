Printer

From the printer's screen: its IP address and access code (in its network / LAN settings) and its serial number (on its Device page). Leave the printer in its normal mode: Bambu's app and cloud keep working. Set this up from your computer (the one running the controller, or one it trusts; not a phone). Reserve the printer's address in your router so it doesn't change.

Screens: printing, paused, done or failed, with a progress ring and the job's name · the time left and when it'll be done · the layer · the nozzle, bed and chamber temperatures · the filament slots, the one in use outlined. It reads the printer directly on your network (nothing goes through Bambu's cloud) and keeps watching even when something else is on the screens.
